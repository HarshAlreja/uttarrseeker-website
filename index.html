About the Founder

Neelam Kukreja

Neelam Kukreja is the founder of Uttarrseeker, a spiritual guidance and healing space created to help people find clarity, encouragement and direction during uncertain phases of life.

Her Story

Born and brought up in Nashik, Neelam holds a post-graduate degree in Nutrition & Dietetics and spent nearly 13 years working in the corporate world before stepping into spiritual guidance full-time. Her fascination with Tarot began during her college years, but it was in 2014 that she finally found a mentor who introduced her to Tarot as a powerful tool for guidance and self-discovery. What began as curiosity gradually grew into a passion and eventually became Uttarrseeker.

Her Philosophy

Neelam believes that guidance should be practical, healing should feel supportive, and spirituality doesn't have to be complicated. Her approach focuses on creating a comfortable and encouraging space where Tarot, healing and spiritual practices can be used as tools for reflection, clarity and personal growth. Through Uttarrseeker, she hopes to help people navigate moments of uncertainty with greater awareness, confidence and direction.

Experience

With years of experience working with Tarot and conducting healing sessions, Neelam's work focuses on spiritual guidance, Tarot readings and holistic healing practices. Her background in Nutrition & Dietetics and her creative personality also shape the way she approaches wellness - with curiosity, practicality and a belief that personal growth can take many forms. Outside of her spiritual practice, Neelam enjoys fitness, experimenting with healthier versions of comfort food, and exploring art and craft - passions that continue to be an important part of who she is.

Our Story

The Story of UttarrSeekar

Uttarrseeker was born in August 2021 as an idea close to the heart, and in August 2023, it became a full-time journey dedicated to spiritual guidance and healing. What started with a desire to help people find clarity gradually grew into a space where seekers could feel safe, supported and free from judgment.

What We Believe

We believe that everyone has the strength to find their own path, but sometimes a little clarity, healing and support can make the journey easier. At Uttarrseeker, guidance is approached with empathy and understanding, because every person's journey is different.

What Uttarrseeker Offers

Uttarrseeker is a space for those seeking guidance, healing and a deeper understanding of themselves — whether you're navigating relationships, career decisions, emotional challenges or simply a period of uncertainty. Our aim is simple: to help you find clarity, heal and move forward with confidence. Welcome to Uttarrseeker — a space where your journey matters.
